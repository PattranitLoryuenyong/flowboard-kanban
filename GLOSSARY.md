# Glossary
- Board: a private collection of tasks owned by one user and optionally shared with members.
- Card: a task with a title and one of three statuses.
- Status: todo (To do), doing (Doing), or done (Done).
- Owner: a board creator, who controls its membership.
- Member: a registered user permitted to work on a shared board.
- Admin: a trusted operator who can list users and suspend/restore their application access.
